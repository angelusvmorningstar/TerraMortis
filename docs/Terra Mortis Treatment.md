TERRA MORTIS TREATMENT

The Scramble for Sydney

Sydney lies empty of monsters. For decades, Australia has been a dead zone to the undead,

but now the barrier weakens. You are a vampire: one of the first sent to claim this virgin

territory. Perhaps young enough to be expendable, ambitious enough to seize opportunity.

Or an Elder, recently awakened from torpor, blood newly thinned, seeking new territory

where your control may flourish.

In this live action roleplay game, you are a monster hiding amongst six million mortals. By

night you hunt for blood, build power, and compete with other vampires for control of the

city. There are no established rulers here, no restrictions except the Traditions, no

expectations besides a handful of social norms, and no laws but those you can enforce. Just

raw opportunity and fierce competition as multiple factions scramble for dominance.

Territory control drives the game: claim hunting grounds through influence and

intimidation, contest rivals' holdings through social manipulation or direct confrontation.

Every zone you control builds prestige; every confrontation risks torpor: a death-like sleep

that removes you from play. Forge alliances that last only as long as they serve your ascent

to power.

This is PVP machination; politics, skulduggery and social manoeuvring wrapped in gothic

horror. You and your rivals carve up Sydney whilst maintaining your disguise amongst the

living. Tonight's ally is tomorrow's rival, and whoever claims domain by right of might, be it a

democratic senate, a despotic prince, or a decadent council of oligarchs, will shape this new

society for centuries to come.

The question isn't whether you'll betray or be betrayed, but whether you'll rule when the

blood settles.

Decades ago a phenomenon known as the Dead Zone swept across Australia. Every vampire

\- from ancient Elders to newly Embraced neonates - simply ceased to exist. Ghouls withered

and died. The continent became a land without vampires overnight.

The phenomenon is well understood by vampire scholars, though beyond our power to

control or reverse. What matters now is that it has weakened enough to allow those with

severely diminished blood to survive entry.

Kindred have been absent from these shores for most of a century. And without Kindred

around to meddle in Mortal affairs, a most remarkable thing has happened - the humans

have made their society secure. The police are, by and large, not corrupt. The street lights

work, and broken lamps are replaced swiftly. The city is now safe to walk alone at night.

CCTV and security guards are everywhere, and the Kine rest easy.

But safe for Mortals is dangerous for Kindred. So Global Covenant Elders have been flexing

their influence to start making the place _just unsafe enough_. Mortal forerunners have spent

recent months establishing basic networks: safe houses, contacts, information flows. The

groundwork is laid. Small areas of Sydney are now dark and grimy enough to make a home

for the All Night Society. Now you've been sent to claim actual power and establish control

over Sydney. Your sponsors have invested considerable resources in this opportunity.

Unfortunately, you're not the only ones with designs on this prize. Multiple factions are

arriving simultaneously, and everyone has the same goal: control of Australia's greatest city.

**Focus on vampire politics**: Other supernatural creatures exist in Australia and they've had a

decade to establish themselves. Keep your attention on vampire business, and they'll likely

ignore you. Go looking for trouble with werewolves or mages, and you'll find yourself

outmatched.

**Politics without power**: The barrier means even ancient Elders must enter with severely

weakened blood. Traditional hierarchies based on mystical power don't apply when

everyone's constrained to similar levels. Age and cunning still matter, but raw supernatural

might doesn't. Expect a dynamic, rapidly shifting political landscape.

**No Elysium exists**: There's no established neutral ground for vampiric society. If you want an

Elysium, you'll need to work together to create one - and then decide who controls it.

**You're not alone**: Player characters represent the Court, not the entirety of Sydney's

vampire population. Your Covenant and Clan Status reflects how many unnamed vampires

follow your lead. Building influence means gathering followers, not just impressing other

player characters.

**Four Covenants only**: The initial scramble is between Invictus, Carthians, Lancea et

Sanctum, and Circle of the Crone.

**No established order:** Sydney has no Prince or Senate, no recognised Sheriff, no Harpy to

enforce Status. There isn't even an Elysium: if you want neutral ground for vampiric society,

you'll need to collectively establish it. Every position of authority must be claimed and

defended. Every territorial boundary, drawn and held.

**Factional envoys with personal agendas:** Your sponsors sent you to advance their interests,

but they're not here to micromanage. When opportunity conflicts with loyalty, which will

you choose? When your faction's goals clash with your coterie's survival, where do your true

allegiances lie?

**Kindred vs Kindred:** The real threats aren't external monsters: they're sitting across the

table from you. Every negotiation could be your breakthrough or your downfall. Every

alliance carries the seed of future betrayal.

**Founder of the new order:** The first Regime of Sydney will be chosen by those in this room.

The laws that govern vampire society here will be written by your actions. The mistakes you

make will become cautionary tales. The victories you claim will become founding legends.

**System**: Vampire: The Requiem 2nd Edition

**Method**: Approved digital dice roller apps

**Source Materials**: Drawing from VtR 2e, Chronicles of Darkness 2e, Hurt Locker, Secrets of

the Covenants, and Night Horrors: Spilled Blood. Content from these books is being curated

for our needs. Some merits’ availability and cost have been changed.

**Rules Consolidation**: A comprehensive PDF is being developed that will consolidate all rules

used in Terra Mortis, with clear notation where our rules differ from the published

materials.

**Territory:** Claimable assets with stats that generate influence, part of a two-tier

**Influence:** Tangible currency distributed each game that must be spent or lost, used

for claims and power plays

Adjusting or removing rules that don't translate well to live play:

●

Rebalanced combat mechanics and non-Kindred fighting styles for vampire-vs-

vampire conflicts

●

Cruac and Theban casting methods being changed to work in LARP context

●

Removal of mechanics with negligible LARP impact

●

2nd edition powers are notably more powerful than 1st edition equivalents

●

Content converted based on theme and concept, not mechanical parity

●

Converted abilities will generally be weaker in 2e context than originally

●

All conversions require ST approval and collaboration

●

Bloodlines: Available with above guidelines, and custom disciplines become

devotions (Players must justify the conversion work)
